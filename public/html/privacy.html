Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 11, 2025

At SecureSync, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our real-time tracking and location services.

1. Data We Collect:

We collect the following types of data:

  • Location Data: We collect real-time location data (latitude and longitude) when you enable tracking. This data is used to display your position on the map and provide tracking services.
  • Chatbot Interactions(Upcoming Features): If you interact with our embedded chatbot (powered by Chatbase), we may collect your messages and responses to improve the chatbot’s functionality.
  • Device Information: We may collect information about your device, such as browser type and IP address, to ensure compatibility and security.

2. How We Use Your Data:

Your data is used to:

  • Provide real-time tracking and location services.
  • Enhance user experience through chatbot interactions.
  • Improve our services and ensure security.

3. Data Sharing:

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • With Chatbase(upcoming feature to enhance safety), to power our chatbot (subject to their privacy policy).
  • As required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Data Security:

We use encryption (HTTPS and WSS) to protect your data during transmission. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights:

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of tracking at any time (via the opt-out page).
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us.
  • Access the data we have collected about you.

6. Compliance with Laws:

We comply with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR and CCPA. If you are a resident of the EU or California, you have additional rights regarding your data.
